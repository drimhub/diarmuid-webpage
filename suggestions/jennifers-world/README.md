# Jennifer's world

Bunnies 🐰 and pictures of drim rain down around Jennifer. Tap one and it flies to her for a kiss 💋; a counter tracks kisses for the current visit.

- With no pictures loaded, drim is a small cartoon face.
- "Add your own drim pictures" lets a visitor pick images from their device; they are shown in the rain via temporary in-page links and never uploaded.

Nothing is saved or shared between visitors. Keeping a high score or shared pictures would need a backend.
