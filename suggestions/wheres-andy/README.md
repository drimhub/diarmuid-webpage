# Where's Andy?

An unlabelled map of the UK and Ireland. Click anywhere that isn't London and you get "No....". Click London and you get "There he is! There's Andy!".

- The map is a rough hand-drawn outline, drawn in SVG from a few coordinates in `script.js`.
- "Play again" resets it.
- Nothing is saved or shared.

Files: `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`.
