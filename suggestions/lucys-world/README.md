# Lucy's world

A pastel meadow full of sparkles where young bulls and feral hogs (pixel-art sprites) wander around and play nicely together.

- Animals drift around the meadow and trade 💕 when they meet.
- Now and then each animal says something: "I'm a young bull!", "I'm a feral hog!", "We love you Lucy!" or "Reading is fun!".
- Tap an animal to make it hop in a shower of sparkles.
- Emoji buttons send floating emoji and keep a count for the current visit.

Nothing is saved or shared between visitors, so there is no comment box. A real community space would need a backend.
