Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026. This is a small personal project run by Diarmuid Coffey (see diarmu.id). It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • We never store your address, postcode or exact location. You choose a neighbourhood (like "Brixton"), and only its name is kept.
  • Other people in an event see your first name, neighbourhood and how you're travelling. Nobody sees your email.
  • Events are deleted 30 days after they happen, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.
  • No adverts, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data.

What we store

  • Your Google account when you sign in: its ID, your name, email address and profile picture link. We show your first name to people in your events. The email address and picture are not shown to anyone or used for anything yet.
  • Events you create or join: the title, type, time and any extras (like outdoor seating); your neighbourhood; whether you're using public transport, cycling or walking; and, if you set one, the longest journey you'd accept.
  • People an organiser adds: an organiser can add a friend who doesn't sign in. We then store the name, neighbourhood, travel mode and optional longest journey that the organiser typed for them. That person never sees it (they have no account), others in the event see their name, neighbourhood and mode, and it is deleted with the event.
  • Suggestions: when the organiser presses "Find a spot", the places suggested and each person's journey time to them (by first name). Place details such as names, addresses, ratings and opening hours come from Google Maps.
  • A sign-in cookie so you stay signed in (30 days). It's essential, so there's nothing to accept. Google's sign-in and Cloudflare's bot check may set their own cookies as part of their services.

What we don't store

Your home address, postcode, or device location. Your device works out the neighbourhood and sends us only that. If you type a postcode, your browser looks it up with postcodes.io directly, so they see the postcode and we don't. If you press "Use my location", your browser's location stays on your device.

Who can see what

  • Anyone with an event link (once signed in): the title, time, the organiser's first name, and how many people have joined.
  • People who have joined: everyone's first name, neighbourhood and travel mode, plus the suggestions and journey times. Others never see the number you set for your longest journey. (If no place fits everyone's limits, the page can say that a place was too far for you by name, without saying what your limit is.)

Other services involved

  • Google for signing in, and for Google Maps Platform, which finds places and works out journey times. To do that we send the central point of each neighbourhood (never your exact location) and the places' positions.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database, runs the bot check when you create an event, and keeps standard request logs.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors supplied the list of neighbourhoods (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

How long we keep things

  • An event, with everyone's details in it, is deleted 30 days after its start time.
  • Suggestions (including the Google place details) are erased 30 days after they were worked out, even if the event is later.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. An account with no events and no activity is removed after 90 days.
  • Cloudflare may keep database backups for up to 30 days after something is deleted.

Your choices

Press Delete my data on the home page (when signed in) to delete your account, the events you organised, and your place in other people's events. The suggestions for those other events are removed too, because they were worked out with you in them, and the others can simply run it again. If an organiser has added you and you would rather not be in their event, ask them to remove you (they can at any time), or get in touch via diarmu.id. To ask for anything else, get in touch via diarmu.id.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and the home page will say so.
